## 3. Technical SEO built into the demo

- Clean slug URLs with keywords
- Unique `<title>`, meta description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter tags on every page (model SEO fields override defaults)
- `sitemap.xml` (static pages, 20 localities, listings, blog) and `robots.txt` (blocks dashboard, admin, portals)
- JSON-LD: `RealEstateAgent` + `LocalBusiness` (home), `FAQPage` (FAQ, locality and type pages), `BreadcrumbList` (all inner pages), `Product`/`Offer` (listings), `Article` (blog)
- One H1 per page, H2/H3 hierarchy, breadcrumbs, alt text, lazy-loaded images
- Mobile first, deferred JS, minified custom CSS/JS (`manage.py minify_assets`)
- Internal linking: footer "Sell land in <area>" links, related areas, related posts, CTAs to the sell form

## 5. Local SEO and off-page

- NAP (name, address, phone) consistent across site, GBP, JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART
