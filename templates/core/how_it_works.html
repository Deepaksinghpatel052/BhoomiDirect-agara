What happens after you submit

  1. Within 24 hours: a relationship manager calls you to confirm details and documents.
  2. Within 3 days: a field agent visits the property, checks boundaries, road access and ground reality.
  3. Evaluation: our team scores location potential, road access, legal clarity, price vs market, resale demand and nearby development.
  4. Legal verification: title chain, Khatauni, mutation (dakhil kharij), encumbrance and owner ID.
  5. Written offer: you see it in your online dashboard and can accept, reject or counter.
  6. Registry: we book the sub-registrar slot, pay stamp duty and transfer the full amount at registry.

Documents to keep ready

  • Registry / sale deed
  • Latest Khatauni (for agricultural land)
  • Mutation / dakhil kharij order
  • Aadhaar and PAN of all owners
  • Site map / naksha (if any)
