Our model

We are not a listing portal. We are the buyer.

Most land in Agra is sold through chains of brokers. Owners wait months, share commission and face buyers who disappear at the last moment. We started {{ SITE_NAME }} to change that.

We buy plots, agricultural land and farmhouse land directly from owners after a clear evaluation and legal check. Once acquired, we resell through our own channel partner network and digital listings, so our business works only when we pay owners on time.

  • Acquisition: owners submit details online or on WhatsApp
  • Evaluation: location, legal status, price and demand scored by our team
  • Direct purchase: written offer and registry with full payment
  • Resale: verified property sold to buyers through partners and our website
